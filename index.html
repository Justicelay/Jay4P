About the Candidate

Meet Your
Candidate

I am Justice Lay Amanfu, a current BSc Computer Science and Management student and your candidate for SRC President at Southshore University College in 2026.

Having served as Deputy Speaker of the Students Parliament here at Southshore, I know firsthand how student governance operates, and where it falls short.

With over twelve years experience in leadership, eight years leading Possibilities Haven as General Manager, and experience as a Business Development Executive at a tech startup, a university lecturer, and a cybersecurity and data analytics intern, I bring a unique blend of strategic leadership, technical knowledge, and deep commitment to service. I have spent my career building institutions, mentoring people, and solving real problems.

"I am not running for SRC President because of ambition. I am running because I have read our Constitution, all 100 pages and I am committed to restoring constitutional governance that genuinely serves every student at Southshore."

Vision & Manifesto

What I Stand For

A comprehensive plan built on constitutional foundations to deliver real programs, real transparency, and real service to every Southshorian.

🏛️
Constitutional Governance
Oath-first leadership, proper separation of powers, and full compliance with the SRC Constitution. No governing without constitutional authority.
📚
Academic Excellence
Inter-faculty competitions, faculty connect platforms, mentorship programs, and robust support for student academic growth at every level.
❤️
Student Welfare
Health screening, canteen revitalization, improved campus facilities, student loan guidance, and genuine support for every struggling student.
💰
Financial Accountability
Published budgets, independent audits, three-signatory controls, quarterly reports, and zero tolerance for misuse of student funds.
🌐
Technology & Innovation
Annual Tech Fair, innovation competitions, SRC digital presence, faculty connect platforms, and support for student tech startups.
🤝
Inclusion & Empowerment
Gender equality programs, women's empowerment, accessibility for students with disabilities, and a campus culture where every voice matters.

📋 Key Manifesto Commitments

✓

Oath before office — Public swearing-in after the declaration of election result

✓

Separation of powers — Restore full independence of all three SRC councils

✓

6 State of Affairs Addresses — All mandatory transparency reports delivered without fail

✓

Cool Classrooms Initiative — Ceiling fans in every lecture hall on campus

✓

Corporate Sponsorship — Target GH₵200,000+ to reduce burden on student fees

✓

Student Loan Support — Dedicated SRC assistance for every eligible student

✓

Inter-Faculty Championships — Competitions in academics, sports, arts, and innovation

✓

Parliament Visit — Annual educational trip for the full Legislative Council

✓

SRC Canteen Revitalization — Expert-led reform for quality, affordable student food

✓

Health Screening — Expanded health programs including general screening for all students

✓

Faculty Connect Platforms — Mentorship and knowledge-sharing across all levels

✓

100-Day Action Plan — Published checklist so you can hold me accountable from Day 1
